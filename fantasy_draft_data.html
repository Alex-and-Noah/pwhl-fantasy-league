Fantasy Draft Data

For the 2026-2027 season, we are making two changes to changes to the fantasy league regarding fantasy points and fantasy team drafting. First, the new structure for scoring fantasy points is as follows:

🏒 2 pts/goal

⛔ 0.1 pts/blocked shot
✨ 2 pts/win

⛔ 0.05 pts/save

Second, all PWHL players will be assigned a Fantasy Draft Value (FDV), and a Value Cap will be imposed to the fantasy teams that will be drafted this year. The FDV will be computed based on the new fantasy point structure applied retroactively to the 2025-2026 season.

2026-2027 Season

Assigning the FDV

The FDV will be assigned differently based on whether the player is a new rookie in the upcoming season. In each case however, instead of looking at Fantasy Points (FP) earned last year, we look at the Projected Fantasy Points (PFP) earned last year. The PFP are defined as follows:

\[ \text{PFP} = 30 * \frac{\text{FP}}{\text{GP}}, \]

where \(GP:=\) games played.

We take the average number of FP earned per game played and multiply that by the total number of games in a season for one team (30) to estimate how many FP would have been earned if the player played every game of the season.

Non-rookie

If the player is not a rookie, the FDV is is simply the player’s earned PFP last season, rounded to the nearest integer.

Rookie

Assigning FDVs to rookies is done by comparing the draft position of the upcoming rookie cohort with the performance of the rookies last season. Each rookie’s FDV is computed as follows:

\[ \text{FDV} = \text{round}\left(0.6 * \text{PFP}_{\text{max,rookie}} * \frac{\text{N}_\text{rookie} - \text{pos}_\text{draft} + 1}{\text{N}_\text{rookie}}\right), \]

where:

  • \(\text{PFP}_{\text{max,rookie}}:=\) maximum PFP amongst all rookies last year
  • \(\text{N}_\text{rookie}:=\) number of new rookies this year
  • \(\text{pos}_\text{draft}:=\) position of rookie in draft order (integer from \(\{1,\ldots,\text{N}_\text{rookie}\}\))

The factor of 0.6 accounts for the risk of drafting a new rookie to a fantasy team.
